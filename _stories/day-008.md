湖边信号不好，所以手机只剩一个拍照功能。不过我挺喜欢这种原始的感觉的，上次在乌孙古道穿越时是一点信号都没有。

今天是个大晴天，脸被晒爆皮了，胳膊也被晒得生疼。感觉都长皱纹了。这里的紫外线太凶猛了，眼睛都被晒疼了。因为月经还没完全干净，所以没有下水，但去湖里划船了，脚湿了，屁股坐到湿椅子上也湿了。我和他们是两个季节，我是冬季，她们是夏季。我把全部衣服都套身上了，穿着四层衣服和她们站在一起拍照，看起来还比她们瘦几圈。其实她们也冷，身上也起鸡皮疙瘩，但她们就那么冻着，没事儿。我已经被冻得拍照都无法自然微笑了。后来终于觉得有点热了，说有点热，Jess 和 Tom 特别激动地对我说："你终于觉得热了！"

Bri 把 Sofia，Dakota 和我的眼镜放在一起，先戴 Dakota 的眼镜，说好晕。然后戴我的眼镜，直接无语了。我把眼镜擦干净，发现右眼也能看很清楚了。

Tom 划船，拖着 Jess 和我划了一大圈。剩余时间，她们继续下水扑腾，我在岸边看景。树上的松鼠又大又可爱，水里的鸭子在疯狂地抢鱼。

昨天是 Bri 的 15 岁生日，我从国内特意带来的苏州擦脸油和卡片送给 Bri，她非常喜欢。因为森林防火，现在不让点篝火，我打开头灯，她们说我物资准备得很充分。当然了，我可是从小就在野外长大的，户外装备必须得有。黑夜里，大家吃一种东西给 Bri 庆生，啤酒汤里放一勺冰淇淋，据说就是生日蛋糕的作用了。

下午三点多就回家了，返程路上我们去看了孵鱼的地方。据说是在这里把鱼苗孵大，然后放回湖里。湖水很深，最深处据说有 300 feet？

到家我们就开始玩拼图，一个平面拼图已经拼好了，Jess 大吃一惊。据说这个拼图已经摆了很久了，一直没拼出来。但我觉得这并不难拼啊！然后我们又启动了一个新的立体拼图。今天总共得拼了 5 个小时，拼得眼睛都花了。

Tom 说我刷牙很认真，让 Bri 和 Sofia 跟我学刷牙，Sofia 有一颗牙已经坏掉变为铁牙了。

**Day 8: 2024-08-17, Saturday**

The signal at the lake sucked, so my phone was pretty much just a camera. But I actually kinda like that raw feeling. Last time I hiked the Wusun Ancient Road, there was zero signal at all.

Today was super sunny. My face got so sunburned it peeled, and my arms hurt from the sun too. I feel like I'm getting wrinkles already. The UV here is brutal—even my eyes hurt from the sun. My period wasn't completely over yet, so I didn't go in the water, but I did go rowing on the lake. My feet got wet, and then my butt got wet from sitting on a wet chair. I'm in a totally different season from them—I'm in winter, they're in summer. I put on all my clothes, four layers, and stood next to them in photos, and I still looked a few sizes thinner than them. Actually they were cold too and had goosebumps, but they just stayed frozen like that and were fine. I was so cold I couldn't even smile naturally in photos. Later I finally felt a little hot and said I was kinda hot, and Jess and Tom got all excited and said, "You finally feel hot!"

Bri put Sofia's, Dakota's, and my glasses together. She tried Dakota's first and said she felt so dizzy. Then she tried mine and was completely speechless. I cleaned my glasses and realized I could see clearly out of my right eye too.

Tom rowed and pulled Jess and me along for a big loop. The rest of the time they kept splashing around in the water while I watched the view from the shore. The squirrels in the trees were huge and cute, and the ducks in the water were fighting like crazy over fish.

Yesterday was Bri's 15th birthday. I gave her the Suzhou face cream and card I'd specially brought from China, and she really loved them. Because of forest fire prevention, campfires aren't allowed right now. I turned on my headlamp, and they said I was really well prepared. Of course—I grew up outdoors, so outdoor gear is a must. In the dark, everyone ate something to celebrate Bri's birthday—a scoop of ice cream in beer soup. Supposedly it did the job of a birthday cake.

We got home a little after 3 p.m. On the way back we stopped to see the fish hatchery. Supposedly they raise the fish fry here and then release them back into the lake. The lake is really deep—the deepest part is said to be 300 feet?

Once we got home we started on puzzles. We finished a flat puzzle, and Jess was shocked. Supposedly it had been sitting there for ages and nobody had ever finished it. But I didn't think it was hard at all! Then we started a new 3D puzzle. We must've spent 5 hours on puzzles today—my eyes were totally blurry.

Tom said I brush my teeth really seriously and told Bri and Sofia to learn from me. Sofia already has a bad tooth that turned into a metal tooth.
