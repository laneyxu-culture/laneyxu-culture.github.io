昨晚没换睡衣，5 点多就醒了，没睡踏实。天气预报说现在外面零下 20 摄氏度，可一点都不觉得冷，不知是体感温度的原因，还是我体质增强了。

今天穿黑色打底裤和 Jess 送的棉靴，美美地去上学。幸亏穿了雪靴，否则我这全身都废了，这么老深的雪。我的雪靴都快湿了，真的就是路上的雪加水加泥的混合物，随便一脚都能踩到脚腕。太恐怖了！这里为什么没有人铲雪啊？！我在北京从没见过这么深的积雪。

下午放学回家先捡了 13 个鸡蛋，今天还行，鸡们没欺负我。很顺利地捡完都放冰箱里了，一个没碎。赶紧先写会儿作业，18:00 去学校看篮球赛。

晚上回到家，准备继续写作业，结果一下喽，发现地下室水漫金山了。我房间地上的衣服、鞋、作业纸全都漂起来啦！这是啥情况？我赶紧跑上楼，报告 Jess。哦，好吧，家里的地下管道漏了。紧急呼叫 Tom，Tom 马上开车往家赶。他真可怜，昨天刚回去上班，今天就又被叫回来了。

Tom 开车回来还得 3 小时，趁等他的功夫，我先回房间把"战场"收拾一下。老师们给我写的推荐信都湿了，虽然这些本来全部在衣柜里，但衣柜全湿了，所以那些信都湿透了，拿手一碰已经成浆糊了。好在我把我所有的小测都拼命拯救下来了。现在我这里就跟烧书的一样，太恐怖了！

床看起来还行，只不过房间湿度非常大。可能因为我挨着暖气炉的缘故，我在屋里感觉自己跟蒸锅上的馒头似的。水看起来倒不像是污水，但因为屋里的地毯和地本来就不干净，屋外堆的那些前阵子砍回来的木头是脏的，所以显得水有点儿污。

Tom 终于回来了。据说水管埋得很深，2014 年漏过一次，非常严重，直接漏到了胸口。那次是早上，他们去城里借那种很大的水泵，泵了一整天才把那些水给泵出来。但因为地下室不通风，所以潮了很长时间。自从 2014 年漏过以后，就再也没有漏过。这次大概率是因为天气太冷了，管道崩塌了。

天气预报明天有暴雨，所以得抓紧修。具体修理方法是 Tom 在外面的雪地里掘地三尺找漏点，我们三姐妹在家里用水泵抽水，然后往楼上运。台阶上都结冰了，很滑。水泵抽水声音巨大。Tom 挖了两个小时，还没找到漏点。我们姐仨运水已经累的直不起腰了。Bri 还有点小发烧，体力也不行。我想起来以前在国内音乐课上学的劳动号子，嘿吼嘿吼地唱起来。Sofia 唱白雪公主里的 Heigh-oh。我们跟着音乐的节奏，干起了流水线。Bri 把水给 Sofia，Sofia 传给我，我跑出去倒外面雪地里。

凌晨两点，Jess 说漏点找到了，Tom 开始修。可怜的 Tom，在雪地里挖了 6 个小时，毫无怨言。Jess 让我们先睡觉。我的房间已经成了水帘洞，进不去了，所以今天只好先睡客厅沙发。我害怕的是猫、狗都在我身边，瞪着眼睛看着我，可能它们奇怪我是什么怪物。据说明天不用上学了，Jess 给我们请假，理由是家里水管漏了。好吧，这种理由放在中国，老师一定不会相信的。

**Day 186: 2025-02-18, Tuesday**

Didn't change into pajamas last night and woke up around 5-something. Didn't sleep well at all. The weather app says it's minus 20 Celsius outside, but I don't even feel cold. Not sure if it's the feels-like temperature or if my body just got tougher.

Today I wore black leggings and the cotton boots Jess gave me and went to school feeling all cute. Thank god I wore snow boots—otherwise my whole body would've been wrecked. The snow is so deep. My snow boots are almost soaked through. It's literally a mix of snow, water, and mud on the road, and every random step you sink up to your ankles. So scary! Why does nobody shovel snow here?! I've never seen snow this deep in Beijing.

After school I went home and collected 13 eggs first. Today was okay—the chickens didn't bully me. Got them all into the fridge smoothly, not a single one broke. Then I hurried to do some homework. At 18:00 I went to school to watch a basketball game.

When I got home at night I was about to keep doing homework, but the moment I went downstairs I found the basement totally flooded—like, water everywhere. The clothes on my floor, my shoes, my homework papers—all floating! What is going on?! I ran upstairs and told Jess. Oh. Okay. The pipe in the basement leaked. Emergency-called Tom, and Tom immediately started driving home. Poor guy—he just went back to work yesterday and today he got called back again.

Tom needs 3 hours to drive back. While waiting, I went back to my room to deal with the "battlefield." The recommendation letters my teachers wrote me got wet. They were all in the closet, but the closet got soaked, so the letters were completely wet—touch them and they turn into paste. Luckily I fought like crazy and saved all my quizzes. Right now it looks like I'm burning books in here. So scary!

My bed looks okay, but the room is super humid. Maybe because I'm right next to the heater, I feel like a steamed bun on a steamer pot in there. The water doesn't look like sewage, but the carpet and floor in the room weren't clean anyway, and the wood piled outside from the trees they cut a while ago is dirty, so the water looks kinda gross.

Tom finally got back. Supposedly the pipe is buried really deep. It leaked once in 2014 and it was really bad—the water went up to their chests. That time it was morning, and they went into town to borrow a huge pump and pumped for a whole day to get the water out. But because the basement doesn't get ventilation, it stayed damp for a long time. After the 2014 leak, it never leaked again. This time it's probably because the weather got too cold and the pipe collapsed.

The forecast says heavy rain tomorrow, so we gotta fix it fast. The repair method: Tom digs three feet down in the snow outside to find the leak, and we three sisters pump water inside the house and carry it upstairs. The steps are all icy and super slippery. The pump is crazy loud. Tom dug for two hours and still hadn't found the leak. Us three sisters were so tired from carrying water we couldn't even stand up straight. Bri even had a slight fever and had no energy. I remembered the work songs I learned in music class back in China, so I started singing heave-ho, heave-ho. Sofia sang "Heigh-oh" from Snow White. We followed the rhythm and worked like an assembly line. Bri passed water to Sofia, Sofia passed it to me, and I ran outside and dumped it in the snow.

At 2 a.m. Jess said they found the leak and Tom started fixing it. Poor Tom—dug in the snow for 6 hours without a single complaint. Jess told us to go to sleep first. My room has basically become a waterfall cave—can't even go in—so today I have to sleep on the living room couch. What I'm scared of is the cat and dog both being right next to me, staring at me like they're wondering what kind of monster I am. Supposedly we don't have to go to school tomorrow—Jess is asking for leave for us, and the reason is the pipe at home leaked. Yeah, okay, in China the teachers would definitely never believe that reason.
