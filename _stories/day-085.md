11 点多起床。和 Bri, Sofia 玩电子手柄游戏，高尔夫，保龄球。计划明天打网球游戏。

今天我来处理万圣节的那些南瓜，把它们变为南瓜馅饺子。决心挺大，结果从下午三点开始，包饺子，包到晚上七点半。原因是家里没有包饺子用的面粉，所以我只好上网查方法。结果发现往面粉里加蛋清和面，竟然成功把低筋面粉变为高筋面粉。可惜饺子馅有点干。因为家里没有猪肉，她们又不吃肥肉，所以用的是鸡胸肉。这是我第一次自己独立包饺子，鸡肉南瓜馅。鸡肉是我自己放搅碎机里搅的肉馅，面是自己和的。面开始回弹很大，包到后面时松弛了。Sofia 喜欢做饭，她跟我一起，我俩干得如火如荼。馅虽然包得不像家里妈妈包的那种很大的馅，但个个挺实的，像模像样。

晚上终于包完了，煮熟后我抢着先把破的饺子先吃了，Jess 拍照留念。味道有点淡，但她们特别喜欢吃。我吃了 10 个。她们分别吃了四五个，不舍得吃了，剩下的都冷冻起来了。

今天画完了一幅江山图，还题了字，很享受。

**Day 85: 2024-11-02, Saturday**

I got up after 11. I played controller games with Bri and Sofia—golf and bowling. We plan to play a tennis game tomorrow.

Today I was going to deal with the Halloween pumpkins and turn them into pumpkin-filled dumplings. I was pretty determined, but in the end I started making dumplings at 3 p.m. and kept going until 7:30 p.m. The reason was that we didn't have dumpling flour at home, so I had to look up methods online. It turned out that adding egg whites to the flour and kneading it successfully turned low-gluten flour into high-gluten flour. Unfortunately, the filling was a bit dry. Since there was no pork at home and they don't eat fatty meat, I used chicken breast. This was my first time making dumplings independently—chicken and pumpkin filling. I minced the chicken myself in the food processor and kneaded the dough myself. At first the dough was very springy, but by the time I got to the later batches it had relaxed. Sofia likes cooking, and she made them with me; the two of us worked away in full swing. Although the filling wasn't as generous as the big ones my mom makes at home, each dumpling was firm and looked decent.

In the evening I finally finished. After boiling them, I rushed to eat the broken ones first, and Jess took photos to mark the occasion. The taste was a bit bland, but they liked them very much. I ate 10. They each ate four or five and couldn't bear to eat more, so the rest were all frozen.

Today I also finished a landscape painting and added an inscription—I really enjoyed it.
