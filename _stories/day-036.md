昨晚几乎没睡，因为今天要去打猎，打鹿。我又害怕，又好奇。据说家里有五把枪，还有很多盒子弹，都在地下室，也就是在我房间隔壁。今天我会拿着一把枪，打树。哈哈，这已经是我能做到的极限了。我真的不敢也不能打动物。虽然我喜欢吃牛肉，但我不敢杀生。

早上 5:40 我全副武装，按要求穿戴橙色的，他们说因为鹿看不出来橙色，但人能看出来。Tom 说这里是无痛打猎，打的位置非常精准，猎物不会感到痛就死了。而且带宝宝的鹿不打，小鹿不打。这里是持证打猎，Tom 和 Bri 都有执照，所以是合法的。Jess 和 Sofia 害怕打猎，所以她们今天不去，但是她们吃猎物。我只是去看，不吃，但可以舔。

准备好了一个欢快的歌单，路上听。还带了点儿香水一类的东西，怕有血腥味。Tom 说要等快天亮才上山，于是我先在屋里画了会儿画，热得满身大汗，又脱衣服。6:30 终于出发了，趁太阳升起的功夫，Tom 开着地形车带我和 Bri 上山。Bri 背着猎枪，我在后面跟着。

沿途看见了很多鹿，但都不是目标群体。枪是不能乱开的，否则以后鹿就不在这附近活动了。整个打猎过程 4 个多小时，一直在找鹿，而且要走到鹿前面去，不能在后面袭击。结果我们一发子弹都没费，无功而返。我也没能打树，因为只有打到鹿后才能开枪打树，否则打树的枪声会吓到鹿的。看来收获猎物并不容易。

我心里窃喜。最高兴也最矛盾的应该就是我了。我既怕猎物被伤害，又不想让猎人失望。

中午时回到家，先补了一觉。下午 Jess 做罐头，今年的收成不好，家里种的西红柿都没结下来，所以此前 Jess 买了很多西红柿，准备做罐头。方法是先把西红柿烤了，然后和洋葱搅拌在一起，罐装，放高压锅里 20 分钟，然后就做好了一罐罐头。Jess 负责准备，Bri 负责点火，我负责看高压锅，每锅 20 分钟。20 分钟的间隙正好够画一幅画，于是我一边看锅一边画画，画了两幅狐尼克。哈哈，很开心！

**Day 36: 2024-09-14, Saturday**

I barely slept last night because today I'm going hunting—for deer. I'm scared and curious at the same time. Apparently there are five guns and lots of boxes of bullets in the basement, which is right next to my room. Today I'll be holding a gun and shooting at trees. Haha, that's literally the limit of what I can do. I really don't dare to and can't shoot animals. I like eating beef, but I can't kill anything.

At 5:40 a.m. I got fully geared up and wore orange as required. They say deer can't see orange, but people can. Tom said it's painless hunting here—the shot placement is super precise, so the animal dies without feeling pain. And they don't shoot does with babies or fawns. It's licensed hunting—Tom and Bri both have licenses, so it's legal. Jess and Sofia are scared of hunting, so they're not going today, but they eat the game. I'm just going to watch and not eat it, but I can lick it. Haha.

I got an upbeat playlist ready to listen to on the way. I also brought some perfume stuff because I was worried about the smell of blood. Tom said we'd wait until it was almost dawn to go up the mountain, so I drew in the house for a while first. I got so hot I was sweating all over and took off some clothes. At 6:30 we finally set off. While the sun was rising, Tom drove me and Bri up the mountain on the ATV. Bri had the rifle on her back, and I followed behind.

Along the way we saw a lot of deer, but none were the ones we were targeting. You can't just shoot randomly, or the deer won't stay around here anymore. The whole hunt lasted over four hours. We kept looking for deer, and we had to get ahead of them—we couldn't attack from behind. In the end we didn't fire a single shot and came back with nothing. I didn't get to shoot at trees either, because you can only shoot at trees after you've got a deer; otherwise the gunshots would scare the deer. So it seems getting game isn't easy.

I secretly felt glad inside. The happiest and most conflicted person there was probably me. I was scared the animals would get hurt, but I also didn't want the hunters to be disappointed.

At noon we got home, and I took a nap first. In the afternoon Jess made canned tomatoes. The harvest wasn't good this year—the tomatoes we grew at home didn't really produce—so Jess had bought a lot of tomatoes earlier and was going to can them. The method is: roast the tomatoes first, then mix them with onions, put them in jars, and pressure-cook them for 20 minutes, and then one batch of canned tomatoes is done. Jess was in charge of prep, Bri was in charge of the fire, and I was in charge of watching the pressure cooker, 20 minutes per batch. The 20-minute gap was just enough to draw one picture, so I watched the pot and drew at the same time. I drew two pictures of Nick Wilde. Haha, I was so happy!
