上午在家闲逛，这里真是世外桃源的感觉，眼前没有高楼林立，远处的山，近处的鹿，空气都是甜的。我家的院子很美。感觉天要下雨，但没下。有点阴冷。

下午去 Jess 单位了，是一个离家不远的诊所，类似社区医院的那种单位。检查了视力，又听了心脏说没事儿；让蹲着在地上走，也正常；让单脚跳，也没问题。这就算通过检查，取得打排球资格了。Jess 说以后如果生病就来这里看。

然后出发去湖边，Jubilee Lake Campground，是一个国家森林公园。顺路接了 Bri 的男友，Dakota。

Tom 开着拖挂式房车，后面带着四个独木舟（canoe）和 2 个桨板。我和 Bri 睡上铺，Sofia 睡下铺，另一张下铺空着放东西。狗睡房车沙发。Dakota 睡房车外的帐篷里。为了怕他冷，把狗垫子都扔给他。

Tom 负责做饭。

**Day 6: 2024-08-15, Thursday**

Chilled at home this morning. It honestly feels like some hidden paradise here—no tall buildings, mountains far away, deer close by, and the air even smells sweet. Our yard is really nice. I thought it was gonna rain, but it didn't. A little cold and gray.

In the afternoon I went to Jess's work. It's a clinic close to home, like a community hospital. They checked my eyesight and listened to my heart and said it was fine. Had me squat-walk on the floor—normal. Then one-leg hops—no problem. So I passed and got cleared to play volleyball. Jess said if I get sick later, I should come here.

Then we headed to the lake, Jubilee Lake Campground, a national forest. We picked up Bri's boyfriend Dakota on the way. Tom drove the towable RV and pulled four canoes and two paddleboards behind it. Bri and I slept in the top bunk, Sofia took the bottom bunk, and the other bottom bunk was empty for stuff. The dog slept on the RV couch. Dakota slept in a tent outside the RV. So he wouldn't get cold, we threw all the dog pads to him. Tom did the cooking.
