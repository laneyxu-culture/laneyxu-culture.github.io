写作节在波特兰以南的 Woodburn High School 举行，是第 40 届俄勒冈写作节（40th Annual Oregon Writing Festival）。

我和校长女儿一屋，昨晚我俩忘关电视就睡着了。6:20 就起了，她洗澡的功夫我又检查了一下今天要带的东西，15 份作文，双面打印，很沉的一坨。检查了房间，东西都带齐了，去退房。贝儿穿着昨天在 Build-a-Bear 新买的衣服，很精神。

酒店里早餐时听见放的新闻是关税问题。

写作节超级有趣，认识一个大美女，G10（可惜没有合影）。还有一个 Boston，G9 的，以前来过这个写作节。活动很有意思，最开始给一些物品，想象谁拥有这些物品。限时 30 分钟，编故事。我写了一整张纸。第二个 workshop 是一个老头儿让大家把自己的人生经历画成 timeline，给旁边人看。别人（据此）提问题，你再回答。然后你选一句你喜欢的话，作为开头，写一篇文章。可惜时间不够，我没写完。我写的是"我的人生非常坎坷，长得跟股票大盘似的。"

交流环节，我们每个人读自己的作品，然后互相点评。我的作品是最长的。有一个同学点评我写长一点会更有帮助。他们觉得我插叙那块写得挺好的。Jadon 写的一个小说，据说写六年了。Sofia 写的是一个梦，梦都特有哲理，写得跟作家写得似的，用词都很高级。她评价我写得很好，碎片化的感觉有想的感觉。Sofia 写得超级好，惊讶地好。她们写得都很深，很散文，但散又不散。

组织者给每人一个 folder，里面有其他同学提交的作品。她们真的写得很好。跟高水平的人在一起就很高兴。我拿了一些同学的作品，非常欣赏。

现场来的作家是澳大利亚的，她的数学和科学超级好。她的家长是搞艺术的，她认为科学更有发展前途。她大学学核物理，辅修数学和哲学。后来用 6 年写了一本书，有关基因编辑的。她像其他作家一样，并没有发表这本书，而是把它藏起来。她写小说的那会儿没有什么人会把科学和写作联系起来。

作家讲到生物、进化论，都是我非常熟悉的。中国也有灰姑娘的历史。写小说有很多种方法，可以由两个拼接成一个，主题、宗旨一样。灰姑娘和现代纽约联系到一起，但没有人发现它们一样，这个作家做了研究。把希腊神话跟现代技术联系在一起。

作家卖书环节发生了一件感人的事。作家一共有三本已发表的书，每本 12.99 美元，但因为我去晚了，第一本获奖作品卖没了，只有第二本在售。我数了数手里的零钱，只有 12 美元，但其实我忘了数钢镚了。然后我说我只有 12 美元，人家也卖我了。这时一个红衣服白头发老奶奶轻轻走过来，跟我说话。吓我一跳。结果她把第三本书递给我，她说她给我买下了第三本书。我当时蒙住了，从天而降的惊喜，也没有留下老奶奶的联系方式，我只知道她是俄勒冈的一名英语老师。然后我拿着两本书去找作家签名，老奶奶还帮我和作家拍了合影。

我跟作家说我也写了很多小说，但大部分烂尾了。她还挺鼓励我的。我们互留了 email。

这次一共去了四个学生，我是 G10，Joe 是 G7，也是田径队的。校长女儿 G7，另一个女孩是 G6。校长女儿和 G6 女孩非常活跃，我和 Joe 更沉稳一些，所以昨天老师分配我和 Joe 每人带一个活跃女孩睡觉。今天下午写作节结束后老师带大家去逛街，给 40 分钟在 North Face 对面活动，让自由组合。俏马上和 Joe 凑成一组，俩人去逛街了。我给贝儿买了裙子、T恤和鞋，一共 21 美金。Joe 买了香水。"Joe 还给我买了一个肉桂卷。我们俩是好朋友。"

回来坐车还要四五个小时，我喝了一杯咖啡，路上一直在听 AP 世界史串讲。

妈妈后天要住院。我有点害怕，但不敢说。今天有一个钼靶的结果出来了，她说她没事儿，让我安心复习 AP 考试，盼我暑假回家。

晚饭没吃，最近胃不舒服。睡前立个 Flag，明天早起趁热打铁捋历史，做题。

**Day 260: 2025-05-03, Saturday**

The writing festival was at Woodburn High School, south of Portland—the 40th Annual Oregon Writing Festival. I roomed with the principal's daughter, and last night we both forgot to turn off the TV and fell asleep. Woke up at 6:20. While she showered, I double-checked what I needed to bring—15 essays, double-sided, a hefty stack. Checked the room, had everything, went to check out. Belle was wearing the outfit she just bought at Build-a-Bear yesterday and looked super sharp.

At breakfast in the hotel, the news was talking about tariffs.

The writing festival was super fun. Met a super pretty girl, G10 (sadly no photo together). And a Boston, G9, who'd been to this festival before. The activities were really interesting. First they gave us some objects and we had to imagine who owned them. 30-minute time limit, make up a story. I filled a whole page. The second workshop was an old guy who had everyone draw their life experiences as a timeline, show it to the person next to you. They ask questions based on it, you answer. Then you pick a sentence you like and use it as the opening to write a piece. Sadly ran out of time, didn't finish. I wrote: "My life has been super bumpy, looks like a stock market chart."

Sharing session: each of us read our work, then gave feedback. Mine was the longest. One classmate said writing longer would be more helpful. They thought my flashback part was pretty good. Jadon wrote a novel, supposedly been working on it for six years. Sofia wrote a dream—dreams are all philosophical, she wrote like a real author, really fancy words. She said I wrote really well, the fragmented feeling had that "wanting" feeling. Sofia wrote incredibly well—surprisingly well. They all wrote really deep, very prose-like, but loose yet not loose.

The organizers gave everyone a folder with other students' submitted work. They really wrote well. Being around high-level people makes me happy. I took some classmates' work, really admired it.

The visiting author was Australian, super good at math and science. Her parents were artists, but she thought science had more future prospects. She studied nuclear physics in college, minored in math and philosophy. Later spent 6 years writing a book about gene editing. Like other authors, she didn't publish it—she hid it away. When she was writing fiction, not many people connected science and writing.

The author talked about biology, evolution—all stuff I'm super familiar with. China also has a Cinderella history. There are many ways to write a novel—you can stitch two into one, same theme, same purpose. Cinderella connected to modern New York, but nobody noticed they're the same—this author did research. Connected Greek mythology to modern technology.

Something really touching happened during the book selling. The author had three published books, $12.99 each, but because I went late, the first award-winning one was sold out, only the second one left. I counted my change—only 12 dollars, but I forgot to count the coins. So I said I only have 12 dollars, and they sold it to me anyway. Then this old lady in red with white hair quietly came over and talked to me. Scared me. Turns out she handed me the third book—she said she bought it for me. I was totally stunned. Out of nowhere surprise. Didn't get her contact info. I only know she's an English teacher in Oregon. Then I took both books to get signed, and the old lady even took a photo of me and the author.

I told the author I've written lots of novels too, but most are unfinished. She was pretty encouraging. We exchanged emails.

Four students went this time. I'm G10, Joe is G7, also on the track team. Principal's daughter is G7, the other girl is G6. Principal's daughter and the G6 girl are super energetic. Joe and I are more calm. So yesterday the teacher assigned me and Joe to each room with one energetic girl. This afternoon after the festival, the teacher took everyone shopping, gave us 40 minutes across from North Face, let us group up freely. Qiao immediately paired with Joe, the two went off shopping. I bought Belle a dress, T-shirt, and shoes, total 21 bucks. Joe bought perfume. "Joe also bought me a cinnamon roll. We're good friends."

The ride back was still four or five hours. I had a coffee and listened to AP World History review the whole way.

Mom's going into the hospital the day after tomorrow. I'm a little scared but don't dare say it. One mammogram result came out today. She said she's fine, told me to focus on AP exams, hoping I come home for summer break.

Didn't eat dinner. My stomach's been off lately. Before bed I'm setting a flag: get up early tomorrow, strike while the iron's hot, review history, do practice questions.
